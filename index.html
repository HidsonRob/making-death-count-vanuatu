Key Messages

Non-communicable diseases were the main contributor to mortality.

Injuries were the smallest contributor: 14 deaths (2.9%).

Under-1 and under-5 deaths were mainly linked to perinatal conditions.

i

Important: This dataset is not a national total. It includes deaths recorded through Vanuatu National Hospital and mainly reflects Port Vila and surrounding areas.

Source: Making Death Count – Vanuatu: Causes of Death Analysis from the Pacific Regional Cause of Death Coding Service, SPC, 2024.
